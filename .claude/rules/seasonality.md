# Seasonality calendar

> **This file uses a worked example with a seasonal product cycle. Rewrite the calendar + worked examples below for your business cycle.** Every recommendation an agent makes must factor in the current phase, so this file is load-bearing.

The example product below has a tight, predictable annual cycle — adapt to yours.

## Phases

| Months | Phase | Marketing posture |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–May | **Peak** (exam prep intensity ramps to D-day) | Max spend, conversion-focused creative, urgency angles, daily-streak emails |
| Jun–Aug | **Low** (post-exam, summer) | Pull spend back to brand + remarketing only, build content for fall, no new acquisition pushes |
| Sep–Nov | **Enrollment** (new school year, students realize gap) | Awareness + early-bird subs, content-led acquisition, parent-targeted creative |
| Dec | **Bridge** | Holiday gift angles, year-end intent peaks early Jan |

## Implications

- Peak: aggressive bidding, frequency caps loosened, expanded creative testing.
- Low: do not interpret CAC spikes as a problem — interpret them as "expected; reallocate budget."
- Enrollment: parents convert > students; segment messaging accordingly.
- Always YoY-compare same phase, never raw MoM across phase boundaries.

## Budget weight (default)

Approx allocation across the year (€40k total):
- Jan–May: 55%
- Sep–Nov: 30%
- Dec: 8%
- Jun–Aug: 7%

## Worked examples

### Example 1 — CAC spike in low season
**Signal:** July 12. Meta CAC hits €38, up from €22 in May (+73%).
- ❌ Wrong read: "Meta is broken — pause campaigns."
- ✅ Right read: "We're in Low phase. CAC spikes are *expected* (no exam pressure → low intent). Reallocate Meta spend to brand/remarketing only and shift the freed budget to fall-content production." Cite seasonality phase explicitly in the recommendation.

### Example 2 — Comparing across phase boundaries
**Signal:** Operator asks "How did April do vs March?"
- ❌ Wrong read: Pull both months and compare raw numbers — "April revenue +28%."
- ✅ Right read: April is deeper into Peak, March is mid-Peak — same phase, MoM comparison is fine. Report it. But if asked "June vs May" → flag explicitly: "These straddle the Peak→Low boundary; YoY comparison is more meaningful."

### Example 3 — Enrollment phase, parent-targeted creative
**Signal:** September 15. Sub conversions are flat WoW.
- ❌ Wrong read: "Test student-targeted creative urgency angles."
- ✅ Right read: We're in Enrollment phase — parents convert > students for early-bird subs. Brief Meta with parent-targeted creative ("Your child has 9 months — start now, not in February"). Student urgency angles are the wrong tool for this phase.

### Example 4 — Peak season, ad fatigue
**Signal:** April 5. Meta frequency on top adset = 3.1, CTR drops from 1.2% → 0.7%.
- ❌ Wrong read: "Cut spend, audience is saturated."
- ✅ Right read: We're in Peak — pulling spend mid-cycle is wrong. Refresh creative on the same audience instead. Frequency cap loosened in Peak per phase implications, but creative rotation cadence tightens.
